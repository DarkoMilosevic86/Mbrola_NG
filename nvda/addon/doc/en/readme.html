MBROLA NG

Croatian text-to-speech for NVDA with the MBROLA diphone synthesizer and the cr1 voice. Copyright © 2026 Darko Milošević.

Voices are not part of the add-on; they are downloaded with the MBROLA_NG Voice Manager (NVDA menu, Tools). Its Installed tab lists installed voices (Set as current voice, Check for updates, Remove, Install from file); the Available tab lists the voices of the catalog (Install shows the voice license, then downloads and verifies the voice). Voices are stored in %APPDATA%\nvda\mbrola_ng\voices\<voice>\. Without an installed voice MBROLA NG does not appear in the synthesizer list.
