*Last updated: 5 October 2026* · [Hrvatski ispod](#pravila-privatnosti--mbrola-ng)

MBROLA NG is free, open-source text-to-speech software by Darko Milošević,
available for Android, iOS, iPadOS, macOS, NVDA, Windows (SAPI 5) and Linux.
This policy covers all of them.

## Summary

**MBROLA NG does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.**
It has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

## What the app does on your device

- **Text you have read aloud** (by TalkBack, VoiceOver, a screen reader or any
  other app) is converted to speech entirely on your device. It is never sent
  anywhere and never saved.
- **Your settings** (speed, pitch, volume and the other voice settings) and
  the **voices you install** are stored only on your device. They are
  deleted when you remove the voice or uninstall the app.

## Internet use

The app connects to the internet for one purpose only: to **download a voice
when you choose to install one**. The voice files are downloaded directly
from GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`, the
[numediart/MBROLA-voices](https://github.com/numediart/MBROLA-voices)
repository) over an encrypted HTTPS connection. No information about you or
your device is sent by the app in these requests. Like any web server,
GitHub receives the technical data needed to deliver a file (such as your IP
address); this is governed by the
[GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Speech synthesis itself works without an internet connection.

## Permissions (Android)

- **Internet** – only to download voices, as described above.

The app requests no other permissions: no contacts, location, microphone,
camera, files or phone state.

## Children

MBROLA NG collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address
with a new date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy:
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/DarkoMilosevic86/Mbrola_NG/issues) of the
MBROLA NG project.

# Pravila privatnosti – MBROLA NG

*Posljednja izmjena: 5. listopada 2026.*

MBROLA NG je besplatan sintetizator govora otvorenog koda autora Darka
Miloševića, za Android, iOS, iPadOS, macOS, NVDA, Windows (SAPI 5) i Linux.
Ova pravila vrijede za sve te verzije.

## Ukratko

**MBROLA NG ne prikuplja, ne pohranjuje, ne dijeli i ne prodaje nikakve
osobne podatke.** Nema korisničkih računa, oglasa, analitike ni praćenja.

## Što aplikacija radi na vašem uređaju

- **Tekst koji se čita naglas** (TalkBack, VoiceOver, čitač ekrana ili bilo koja
  druga aplikacija) pretvara se u govor isključivo na vašem uređaju. Nikamo se ne
  šalje i nigdje se ne sprema.
- **Vaše postavke** (brzina, visina, jačina i ostale postavke glasa) i
  **instalirani glasovi** spremaju se samo na vašem uređaju. Brišu se kad
  uklonite glas ili deinstalirate aplikaciju.

## Korištenje interneta

Aplikacija se spaja na internet samo zbog jednoga: da **preuzme glas kad ga
odaberete za instalaciju**. Datoteke glasa preuzimaju se izravno s GitHuba
(`raw.githubusercontent.com`, repozitorij
[numediart/MBROLA-voices](https://github.com/numediart/MBROLA-voices)) preko
šifrirane HTTPS veze. Aplikacija pritom ne šalje nikakve podatke o vama ni o
uređaju. Kao i svaki web poslužitelj, GitHub prima tehničke podatke potrebne
za isporuku datoteke (npr. IP adresu), na što se primjenjuje
[izjava o privatnosti GitHuba](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Sama sinteza govora radi bez internetske veze.

## Dopuštenja (Android)

- **Internet** – samo za preuzimanje glasova, kako je opisano gore.

Aplikacija ne traži nikakva druga dopuštenja: ni kontakte, ni lokaciju, ni
mikrofon, ni kameru, ni datoteke, ni stanje telefona.

## Djeca

MBROLA NG ne prikuplja podatke ni od koga, pa tako ni od djece.

## Izmjene

Ako se ova pravila promijene, nova verzija bit će objavljena na istoj adresi
s novim datumom.

## Kontakt

Pitanja o ovim pravilima:
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/DarkoMilosevic86/Mbrola_NG/issues)
