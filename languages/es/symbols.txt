# FORMAT:  character | level | name [| break]
#   level  the symbol is spoken when the punctuation level (host setting)
#          is at least this:  none (always) some most all  never
#          Defaults: SAPI/Android "some", NVDA "none".
#   break  pause/intonation it causes: comma semicolon colon dash period
#          question exclaim paragraph
# Characters may be written as U+XXXX (needed for | and #).
# The opening marks ¿ and ¡ only have a name; the closing ? and ! at the end
# of the sentence give the question and exclamation intonation.
# =============================================================================
